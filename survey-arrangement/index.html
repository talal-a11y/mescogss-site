Survey Arrangement

OUR SERVICES

MESCO GSS offers a wide range of survey arrangement services tailored specifically for shipowners and operators.

  • We coordinate and schedule surveys with qualified marine surveyors to ensure your vessels comply with all regulatory and safety standards.

  • Our survey services include various inspections, such as pre-purchase, condition, damage, and valuation surveys, providing you with detailed and accurate reports.

  • We manage the scheduling of surveys to minimize disruption to your operations, ensuring inspections are conducted efficiently and within the necessary timeframes.

  • We guarantee that all survey reports are comprehensive and detailed, providing clear insights and recommendations to aid your decision-making.

  • We offer ongoing support throughout the survey process, addressing any questions or concerns and ensuring smooth communication among all parties involved.

With our survey arrangement services, MESCO GSS ensures your vessels are thoroughly and professionally inspected, giving you the confidence and information needed to maintain optimal operations.
