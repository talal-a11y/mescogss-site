P&I Club Entry

OUR SERVICES

At MESCO GSS, we specialize in providing comprehensive Protection and Indemnity (P&I) Club entry services tailored for shipowners and charterers.

  • We offer personalized guidance to help you understand the benefits and coverage options available from different P&I Clubs, ensuring we find the best match for your maritime requirements.

  • Our team conducts thorough risk assessments of your vessels to identify potential liabilities and align them with suitable insurance solutions.

  • We streamline the entry process into your selected P&I Club, managing all documentation and compliance obligations on your behalf.

  • Leveraging our industry expertise, we negotiate competitive premiums and favorable terms that fit your budget and coverage needs.

  • We provide ongoing support and guidance throughout your membership, including assistance with claims and policy renewals.

With our dedicated P&I Club entry services, MESCO GSS enables you to navigate the complexities of maritime insurance confidently, ensuring your vessels are protected against a wide range of third-party liabilities.
